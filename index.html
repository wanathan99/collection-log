Data scraped from the OSRS Wiki Collection Log table and refreshed automatically every Thursday. Comp% is the wiki's estimated percentage of WikiSync-tracked players who've obtained that item. Difficulty and hours estimates are a one-time snapshot from the community-maintained Collection Log Adviser spreadsheet (hours = drop-rate attempts ÷ Iron kills-per-hour, on-task rate for slayer bosses), not auto-refreshed, and not available for every item. Items marked with * are gated behind a prerequisite item, so the real time is likely longer than shown. Your checks and notes are stored only in this browser (localStorage) — nothing is uploaded anywhere.
